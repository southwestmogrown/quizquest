# Welcome to QuizQuest

QuizQuest is a gamified learning platform built to make technical skills stick — not just feel understood in the moment.

Most learning platforms give you a video, a wall of text, and a multiple-choice question that you can guess your way through. QuizQuest takes a different approach: **you learn by doing**, with immediate feedback at every step.

## How It Works

Every course is made of three types of lessons:

**Reading lessons** (like this one) lay the groundwork. Short, focused, and direct — no padding, no filler.

**Code challenges** put a live editor in front of you. Write real code, hit Run, and see the output in your browser. When you Submit, the grader checks your work and awards XP. Partial credit is possible — the grader shows you which test groups passed.

**Quizzes** test comprehension. One focused question per lesson. Explanations are shown for every choice — right or wrong — so you always leave knowing more than when you arrived.

## XP and Streaks

Every lesson completed earns XP. Code challenges award more (25 XP) than reading or quizzes (10–15 XP) because they require more effort.

Your streak increments each day you complete at least one lesson. Lose a day, lose your streak. It's a small thing with a surprisingly large motivational effect.

## Sequential Unlocking

Lessons unlock in order. You can't skip ahead — each lesson unlocks the next after you complete it. This is intentional: the platform assumes each lesson builds on the one before it.

## What You'll Do in This Tour

The next three lessons will walk you through every feature:

1. **A live code challenge** — write Go code in the browser, run it, and submit
2. **A deep dive into the AI coach** — what it is, how it works, why it's different
3. **A quiz** — one question about what you've just learned

Start the tour by completing this lesson and unlocking the code challenge.
